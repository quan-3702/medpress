MedPress – How to use

Print a weekly medicine chart, a medicine list for the doctor, and a wallet card.

1. Who the medicines are for

  1. Under Whose medicines, type the person's name, date of birth and any allergies. Allergies are printed in red on every page.
  2. Click ☰ More and then Fill in an example to see a filled-in example first. It replaces what you have typed, so try it before you start.

2. Add each medicine

  1. Click + Add a medicine.
  2. Type the name and how much (for example 500 mg – 1 tablet).
  3. Tick when it is taken: Morning, Midday, Evening, Bedtime, or Only when needed. A medicine taken twice a day gets two ticks.
  4. If you like, add what it is for, notes such as With food, and what the tablet looks like (white oval).
  5. Click Save. Click a medicine in the list to change it, or × to remove it.
Copy the names and amounts from the pharmacy label or the leaflet in the box, exactly as written.

3. Choose what to print

  • Weekly chart: a page for the fridge or bedside, with a box for each dose on each day. Tick the box after taking the medicine, so nobody has to wonder whether it was taken. Choose the week it starts and how many weeks to print.
  • Medicine list: one page with every medicine, how much, when, and what it is for, plus your doctor, pharmacy and emergency contact. Take it to every appointment and hospital visit.
  • Wallet card: a small card to keep in a purse or wallet. Cut along the dotted line and fold in the middle.

4. Contacts

Under Contacts, type the doctor, pharmacy and an emergency contact with phone numbers. They appear on the medicine list, the wallet card and the bottom of the weekly chart.

5. Printing

  1. Choose US Letter or A4 paper. Keep Large print on for easier reading.
  2. Click 🖨 Print. In the print window set Scale to 100% (Actual size) and turn on Background graphics for the coloured headings.
  3. The weekly chart prints sideways (landscape) by itself.

6. More than one person

Caring for a parent and a partner? With Pro, click + Person to keep a separate list for each person and switch between them with the box at the top.

7. Free version and Pro

The free version never limits how often you print.

  • Free: one person, up to 6 medicines, one week at a time, all three print types.
  • Pro (one-time purchase): any number of people and medicines, and up to 4 weeks printed at once.

8. Important

MedPress only prints what you type. It does not check doses or warn about medicines that should not be taken together. Always follow the instructions from your doctor or pharmacist, and ask them if anything is unclear. Update and reprint the list whenever a medicine changes.

9. If something goes wrong

  • The colours are missing: turn on Background graphics in the print window.
  • The wallet card is the wrong size: set Scale to 100% or Actual size.
  • My list is gone on another PC: it is stored only on the PC where you typed it.
